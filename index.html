About Me

Data-Driven Analyst

I'm a Data Analyst who turns raw, messy data into clear business insight. I work end-to-end — pulling and shaping data with SQL and Python, cleaning and modeling it in Excel, and presenting it through interactive dashboards that stakeholders can actually act on.

My day-to-day toolkit centers on SQL for querying and analysis, Python (Pandas/NumPy) for data cleaning and exploration, Excel for modeling and reporting, and Tableau or Power BI for building the dashboards that surface trends, KPIs, and root causes.

I care about the full analytics workflow — from understanding a business question, to writing the query, to designing a dashboard that answers it clearly and drives a decision.
